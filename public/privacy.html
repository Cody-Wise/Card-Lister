Bigfoot Boys Command Center Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 22, 2026

Bigfoot Boys Command Center is a private tool used to prepare, publish, and review sports card listings. The app connects to eBay only after you authorize access through eBay's OAuth flow.

Information the app accesses

When authorized, the app may access eBay account, inventory, listing, offer, and order information needed to create listings, manage listing settings, and review sales activity.

How information is used

Information from eBay is used only inside this app to support listing, pricing, publishing, and sales reporting workflows. The app does not sell personal information.

Storage

OAuth tokens and listing configuration are stored for the purpose of keeping the authorized connection active. You can revoke access from eBay or reset eBay authorization in the app.

Contact

For questions about this app or this policy, contact the app owner who operates this Bigfoot Boys Command Center installation.
